## TL;DR 

 Food shelf life varies dramatically by category and storage condition. Dairy lasts 1-4 weeks refrigerated; hard grains 1-10 years in dry storage; frozen meat 4-12 months at -18 °C. The single most important factor for extending shelf life is temperature control: every 10 °C reduction halves [spoilage](https://dotheygobad.com/microbial-vs-chemical-spoilage-explained/) rates. This database provides shelf life data for all major food categories with scientific context for why shelf life differs within each group.
