## What this address is for

**Corrections.** If an article states something wrong, send the page URL, the claim, and — if you have one — the source that contradicts it. I aim to reply within 7 days and to correct or annotate the page once the point is confirmed. Substantive corrections are noted on the page rather than made silently.

**Editorial questions.** Why an article covers something a certain way, what a term means, or a mechanism you think deserves a page.

**Reuse and quotation.** Short quotation with attribution is fine without asking. Republishing a substantial part of an article requires written permission — email with the page and where it would appear.

**Privacy requests.** Access, correction or deletion of personal data. Details are in the [Privacy Policy](legal/privacy-policy.md); requests are answered within 30 days.

## What I cannot help with

I do not assess whether a specific food is safe to eat, diagnose spoilage from a photograph, provide laboratory testing, or give regulatory determinations for a product or market. Those questions need the actual sample, and the competent authority in your market.

If you are asking whether food is safe: if there is any doubt — visible mould, off-odours, damaged packaging, a broken cold chain — discard it and follow your local food safety authority's guidance.

## Before you write

- **A broken link or a page that will not load** — tell me the URL, it is usually quick to fix.
- **A citation that does not say what the article claims** — this matters more than anything else on the site; send it and it gets checked.
- **Commercial enquiries** (sponsorship, advertising, partnerships) — note that advertising on the site is served by Google AdSense and editorial decisions are independent of it. See [Editorial Principles](editorial-principles.md).
