# Editorial Principles

## How Content Is Created at DoTheyGoBad

At **DoTheyGoBad**, all content is created with a single goal: **to explain food [spoilage](/articles/microbial-vs-chemical-spoilage-explained/) and shelf-life behavior accurately from an [industrial food](/articles/food-science-basics/) science perspective**.

This site does not publish lifestyle advice, cooking tips, or household storage guidance. Instead, every article follows a structured, professional framework rooted in **food processing, formulation, and manufacturing realities**.

## Our Editorial Principles

### 1. Industrial Perspective First

All topics are analyzed through the lens of **industrial food production**, including:

- Ingredient formulation and functional roles

- Standardized processing methods

- Microbiological and chemical stability

- Packaging systems and barrier performance

- Shelf-life design and validation

Household variability is intentionally excluded to ensure explanations remain **generalizable and technically meaningful**.

### 2. Ingredients and Processes Over Opinions

Articles focus on *how food systems behave*, not on opinions or anecdotal experience.

We prioritize:

- What ingredients do at a molecular or functional level

- How processing alters structure and stability

- Why specific spoilage pathways dominate in certain foods

Speculation, fear-based language, and sensational claims are avoided.

### 3. Clear Scope and Content Boundaries

To maintain accuracy and trust:

**We do not:**

- Provide home food storage advice

- Determine whether a food is safe to eat

- Offer medical, dietary, or regulatory guidance

- Replace food safety authorities or official standards

Content is educational, explanatory, and non-prescriptive.

### 4. Evidence-Informed, Experience-Guided

Where appropriate, articles reference:

- Publicly available scientific literature

- Industry standards and common manufacturing practices

- Widely accepted food science principles

Final interpretation is guided by **real-world industry experience**, not automated summaries or unverified claims.

### 5. Responsible Use of AI Tools

AI tools may be used to:

- Locate background information

- Summarize publicly available research

- Improve research efficiency

However:

- Topic selection remains human-led

- All conclusions are reviewed by the author

- Content reflects professional judgment and industrial context

AI does not replace expertise.

### 6. Editorial Independence

DoTheyGoBad maintains strict separation between:

- Educational content

- Any future commercial or B2B services

No paid relationships influence editorial conclusions.

### 7. Corrections and Updates

Food science evolves. When new evidence or clearer explanations emerge:

- Articles may be updated

- Revisions are made transparently

- Accuracy is prioritized over permanence
