# Food Science Glossary (Industrial Perspective)

Every term below is tied to the document that actually defines or governs it: EU food law and US federal regulation for statutory terms, ISO standards for methods and vocabulary, the UK Food Standards Agency's HACCP glossary for hazard terms, and peer-reviewed reviews where the concept is scientific rather than legal.

Where a term has no statutory definition — "formulation" is the clearest example — the entry says so rather than borrowing a definition from a non-authoritative source.

| **Term** | **Definition and where it is defined** |
| --- | --- |
| **Accelerated Shelf Life Testing** | Laboratory method to predict shelf life by storing food under elevated stress conditions (e.g., temperature, humidity) to simulate long‑term degradation. This approach is widely used in industry for faster shelf life estimation. _(Shelf-life prediction from accelerated conditions — review)_ [PMC review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12692103/) |
| **Anaerobic Spoilage** | Spoilage by microorganisms that grow in the absence of oxygen, such as _Clostridium_ species commonly associated with anaerobic conditions in vacuum or modified atmosphere packaging. _(UK Food Standards Agency HACCP glossary)_ [FSA MyHACCP](https://myhaccp.food.gov.uk/help/guidance/glossary) |
| **Antioxidants** | Substances that prevent or slow oxidation in foods, maintaining quality and nutritional value. They are used to delay rancidity and oxidative deterioration. _(Functional class 'antioxidants' — EU Regulation 1333/2008 on food additives)_ [EU Reg 1333/2008](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2008/1333/oj) |
| **ATP Bioluminescence Test** | Rapid method to measure microbial activity in food production environments by detecting adenosine triphosphate (ATP), a marker of live cells. _(ATP-based hygiene assessment — review)_ [PubMed review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32338738/) |
| **Brine / Salting** | Application of salt or a salt solution (brine) to bind water and reduce _(UK Food Standards Agency HACCP glossary)_ [FSA MyHACCP](https://myhaccp.food.gov.uk/help/guidance/glossary) |
| **Cold Chain** | Temperature‑controlled supply chain process that ensures perishable food products are maintained at appropriate temperatures from processing to distribution, essential for quality and safety. _(Temperature requirements for food of animal origin — EU Regulation 853/2004)_ [EU Reg 853/2004](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2004/853/oj) |
| **Cold Pasteurization / High Pressure Processing (HPP)** | Non‑thermal preservation technique applying very high pressure to inactivate microorganisms and enzymes while preserving food texture and nutritional quality. _(High-pressure processing — review)_ [PubMed review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40326610/) |
| **Dehydration / Drying** | Removal of water from food to lower water activity and inhibit microbial growth, a fundamental preservation technique in food processing. _(Drying and dehydration of foods — review)_ [PMC review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3550996/) |
| **Emulsification** | Process of mixing two immiscible liquids (oil and water) into a stable mixture, common in sauces, dressings, and processed foods. _(Functional class 'emulsifiers' — EU Regulation 1333/2008)_ [EU Reg 1333/2008](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2008/1333/oj) |
| **Enzymatic Browning** | Discoloration of foods (especially fruits and vegetables) caused by enzymatic oxidation of phenolic compounds when exposed to oxygen. _(Polyphenol oxidase and enzymatic browning — review)_ [PubMed review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37954915/) |
| **Food Additives** | Substances added to food to improve flavor, texture, appearance, or shelf life, including emulsifiers, stabilizers, preservatives, and antioxidants. _(Statutory definition and functional classes — EU Regulation 1333/2008)_ [EU Reg 1333/2008](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2008/1333/oj) |
| **Foodborne Pathogens** | Microorganisms capable of causing illness in humans, such as _Salmonella_, _Listeria_, and _E. coli_. Control of pathogens is a central focus in food safety systems. _(UK Food Standards Agency HACCP glossary)_ [FSA MyHACCP](https://myhaccp.food.gov.uk/help/guidance/glossary) |
| **Formulation (Food Formulation)** | Designing the ingredient composition of food products to achieve desired quality, safety, and shelf‑life, incorporating factors such as Aw, pH, and preservatives. _(No statutory definition; the nearest legal anchor is the ingredient declaration — EU Regulation 1169/2011)_ [EU Reg 1169/2011](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2011/1169/oj) |
| **Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP)** | Standard protocols ensuring hygienic, safe, and quality food production, foundational to food safety management systems. _(Current good manufacturing practice — 21 CFR Part 117 Subpart B)_ [US eCFR 21 CFR 117](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-117) |
| **Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points (HACCP)** | Systematic preventive approach to food safety that identifies hazards and establishes control measures at critical points in production. _(Food safety management systems — ISO 22000:2018)_ [ISO 22000:2018](https://www.iso.org/standard/65464.html) |
| **Hygiene and Sanitation** | Measures in industrial production (cleaning, sanitization, personnel hygiene) to prevent contamination and ensure safe food handling. _(Hygiene requirements for food businesses — EU Regulation 852/2004)_ [EU Reg 852/2004](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2004/852/oj) |
| **Lipid Oxidation** | Chemical reaction of fats with oxygen leading to rancidity, off‑flavors, and nutrient loss; a major cause of quality deterioration in lipid‑rich foods. _(Lipid oxidation in foods — review)_ [PMC review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10307983/) |
| **Microbial Contamination** | Presence of bacteria, yeast, or mold in foods that can lead to spoilage or foodborne illness, monitored by microbiological testing in industrial systems. _(UK Food Standards Agency HACCP glossary)_ [FSA MyHACCP](https://myhaccp.food.gov.uk/help/guidance/glossary) |
| **Modified Atmosphere Packaging (MAP)** | Packaging technique where air inside a package is replaced with a controlled gas mixture (e.g., reduced O₂, added CO₂/N₂) to slow microbial growth and oxidation. _(Modified atmosphere packaging — review)_ [PubMed review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22063169/) |
| **Nitrogen Flushing** | Replacing air with nitrogen in packaging to reduce oxidation and slow spoilage reactions. _(Packaging gas E941 (nitrogen) — EU Regulation 1333/2008)_ [EU Reg 1333/2008](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2008/1333/oj) |
| **Oxidation‑Reduction Potential (ORP)** | Measure of a food system’s tendency to gain or lose electrons; influences microbial growth, oxidation, and spoilage stability. _(UK Food Standards Agency HACCP glossary)_ [FSA MyHACCP](https://myhaccp.food.gov.uk/help/guidance/glossary) |
| **Oxidative Rancidity** | Spoilage of fats and oils due to oxidation, leading to development of off‑flavors and odors. _(Lipid oxidation and rancidity in foods — review)_ [PMC review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10307983/) |
| **Packaging Materials** | Materials (films, glass, cartons, barriers) used to protect food from moisture, oxygen, and light, essential for shelf‑life preservation. _(Food contact materials — EU Regulation 1935/2004)_ [EU Reg 1935/2004](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2004/1935/oj) |
| **Pasteurization** | Heat treatment process that destroys harmful microorganisms and reduces microbial load without significant quality loss, widely used for milk and juices. _(General definitions, including pasteurization — 21 CFR 1240.3)_ [US eCFR 21 CFR 1240.3](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-1240/subpart-A/section-1240.3) |
| **Preservatives** | Chemical agents added to foods to prevent spoilage by inhibiting microbial growth or oxidation. _(Functional class 'preservatives' — EU Regulation 1333/2008)_ [EU Reg 1333/2008](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2008/1333/oj) |
| **Protein Denaturation** | Structural changes in proteins caused by heat, pH changes, or chemicals, affecting texture and functionality in foods. _(Protein denaturation under food processing — review)_ [PMC review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12152504/) |
| **Protein Hydrolysis** | Breakdown of proteins into peptides or amino acids, influencing flavor, nutrition, or spoilage processes. _(Protein hydrolysates in food — review)_ [PubMed review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36692023/) |
| **pH / Acidity** | Measure of hydrogen ion concentration; acidic foods inhibit many spoilage bacteria and are a classic control factor in preservation. _(Acidified foods, defined by pH — 21 CFR Part 114)_ [US eCFR 21 CFR 114](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-114) |
| **Sensory Analysis** | Testing food characteristics (taste, smell, texture, appearance) using human sensory panels to assess quality attributes. _(Sensory analysis — vocabulary, ISO 5492:2008)_ [ISO 5492:2008](https://www.iso.org/standard/38051.html) |
| **Shelf Life** | Period during which a food remains safe and retains desired sensory, chemical, and physical properties under specified conditions. _(Date of minimum durability — EU Regulation 1169/2011 on food information to consumers)_ [EU Reg 1169/2011](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2011/1169/oj) |
| **Spoilage Indicators** | Chemical, physical, or microbial signs that a food is deteriorating (odor, discoloration, gas formation) used in quality monitoring. _(Detection of food spoilage — review)_ [PubMed review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40717620/) |
| **Sterilization** | Intensive heat or equivalent treatment that destroys all microorganisms, significantly extending shelf life for canned or aseptic products. _(Thermally processed low-acid foods, commercial sterility — 21 CFR Part 113)_ [US eCFR 21 CFR 113](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-113) |
| **Vacuum Packaging** | Removal of air from packaging to limit oxygen‑dependent microbial growth and oxidative reactions. _(UK Food Standards Agency HACCP glossary)_ [FSA MyHACCP](https://myhaccp.food.gov.uk/help/guidance/glossary) |
| **Water Activity (Aw)** | Measure of free water available for microbial growth; controlling Aw is critical for shelf life — reducing Aw inhibits most bacteria. _(UK Food Standards Agency HACCP glossary)_ [FSA MyHACCP](https://myhaccp.food.gov.uk/help/guidance/glossary) |
