## Science Principles Behind Food Stability

Every article explains *why* a food spoils through the lens of core food science principles:

| Principle | Impact on Shelf Life | Key Parameter |
|---|---|---|
| **Water Activity (aw)** | Available water for microbial growth | aw < 0.60 = microbiologically stable |
| **Lipid Oxidation Kinetics** | Rancidity rate predictions | Doubles every 10 °C temperature rise |
| **pH & Acidification** | pH < 4.6 blocks pathogen growth | Botulism threshold at pH > 4.6 |
| **Enzymatic Activity** | PPO, lipase, protease degrade quality | Slowed but not stopped by freezing |
| **Hurdle Technology** | Combined preservation = synergistic effect | Multiple weak hurdles > one strong one |
