About DoTheyGoBad — The Food Shelf Life Science Encyclopedia
============================================================

Our Mission
-----------

DoTheyGoBad is a **food shelf life science encyclopedia** dedicated to explaining why foods spoil from an [industrial food](/food-science-basics/)
 science and processing viewpoint. We bridge the gap between academic food science and practical shelf-life knowledge — providing clear, scientifically grounded explanations that reflect real-world industrial food chemistry, microbiology, and preservation engineering.

Unlike most food websites that focus on home storage tips and culinary practices, our content is built on the **standardized principles of industrial food manufacturing** — where ingredient composition, processing methods, and packaging systems create predictable [spoilage](/microbial-vs-chemical-spoilage-explained/)
 patterns that can be systematically analyzed.

Our Perspective: Industrial Food Science First
----------------------------------------------

Every topic on DoTheyGoBad is viewed through the lens of industrial food science. We focus on:

*   **Ingredient composition** and functionality — [water activity](/water-activity-food-stability/)
     (aw), pH, fat/protein content
*   **Processing methods** and their effects on microbial and chemical stability
*   **Spoilage mechanisms** — microbial growth kinetics, lipid oxidation, enzymatic deterioration
*   **Packaging systems** — barrier properties, MAP, vacuum packaging
*   **Shelf-life design** — testing protocols, Q10 factors, accelerated studies
*   **Preservation technology** — hurdle concept, thermal processing, cold chain management

Who This Site Is For
--------------------

*   **Food industry professionals** — QA/QC teams, production managers, R&D scientists
*   **Product developers** — designing stable formulations with targeted shelf life
*   **Procurement specialists** — evaluating ingredient and product stability
*   **Food science students** — applied reference for spoilage mechanisms and preservation
*   **Scientifically curious readers** — who want to understand the real chemistry behind food spoilage

Why This Site Exists
--------------------

Most food information online emphasizes home storage tips like “keep it in the fridge” or “smell it to check.” These are useful but don’t address **how and why** standardized industrial products behave the way they do. Modern food supply chains, ingredient interactions, and processing technologies create complex environments where stability is determined by chemistry, microbiology, and engineering — not guesswork.

DoTheyGoBad fills that gap — offering the scientific framework that explains why bread stales, why butter goes rancid, why eggs spoil, and how preservation technology extends shelf life in the real world.
