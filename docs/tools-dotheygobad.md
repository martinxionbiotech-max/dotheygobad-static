# Food Science Tools

Two calculators, both built to answer a question the articles describe in prose. They are deliberately narrow: each returns a number you can check by hand, and each links back to the page explaining the principle behind it.

## NRV Calculator

[Open the NRV calculator](https://dotheygobad.com/nrv-calculator/)

Nutrient Reference Values are the reference intakes used on food labels — the "% NRV" column. The calculator converts a measured nutrient content per 100 g or per serving into the percentage a label has to declare.

Two caveats matter in practice. NRVs differ by jurisdiction (the EU, US and China use different reference values for several vitamins and minerals), and the calculation is only as good as the analytical result behind it. A label claim derived from a formulation rather than a test is an estimate, and regulators treat it as one.

## Water Safety Risk Calculator

[Open the water safety risk calculator](https://dotheygobad.com/water-safety-risk-calculator/)

A structured risk assessment for water used in food production or as an ingredient: source, treatment, storage and distribution each contribute to the final risk band.

The output is a screening tool, not a substitute for testing. It is useful for prioritising which water streams need microbiological sampling, and for documenting a due-diligence review — which is often what an auditor asks for.

## How to use these

Neither tool replaces a laboratory result or a regulatory decision. Use them to sanity-check a label, to prioritise sampling, or to explain a calculation to a colleague; then verify the underlying number against your own analysis.
